# Question and earlier work

Per Alexandersson's public conjecture asks for real-rootedness of the ordinary-descent polynomials of permutations with shortest cycle length at least k, for 3≤k≤n. His 26 November 2026 problem page records exact tests up to k=6 and n=22. His other conjecture in that section uses strict excedance and asks about consecutive sizes. The present theorem uses ordinary descents and permits mixtures across cutoffs at one fixed size. [Problem statement](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#permutations-with-long-cycles)

Alexandersson already proved the derangement case k=2 for all n in Theorem 2.1. The unrestricted k=1 case is the classical Eulerian theorem. The new compatibility result includes arbitrary nonnegative weights on all the cutoffs, including these two. Alexandersson's failures of palindromicity at some cutoffs and his examples about *consecutive* ordinary-descent rows are consistent with the theorem here. [Alexandersson, arXiv:2609.07325v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07325v2)

ProgramComputer’s 25 November 2026 manuscript considers the same ordinary-descent rows. It proves that for n≥25 and 52 log n ≤ k ≤ n, every nonzero nonnegative combination of the individual admissible cycle-type polynomials has degree n−2 and simple nonpositive zeros. It also proves the single-cycle case at every size, including the double zero at −1 for n=5. That theorem allows a separate weight for each type within its size range. This release permits weights on whole cutoff rows and covers every size and cutoff, including fixed k≥3 at arbitrarily large n. It adds common isolation for all long-cycle rows at each size and the exact two exceptions for mixtures admitting k=1,2. [ProgramComputer, Theorems 1 and 2](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/blob/4da48b72ed8f92f96c2faf60e2d63d253b3b507a/topics/long-cycle-descents/paper.tex)

The primitive-necklace identity comes from Gessel and Reutenauer. Elizalde and Troyka give exact and asymptotic descent-set counts for n-cycles. Gessel and Zhuang give enumerative refinements by cycle type. These formulas supply input to the root-location proof. [Gessel–Reutenauer](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/009731659390095P) · [Elizalde–Troyka](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05103) · [Gessel–Zhuang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.00654)

Kevin Liu and Mei Yin showed that low-order descent moments on a conjugacy class agree with those of the whole colored permutation group when short cycles are absent. They also obtained asymptotic normality under long-cycle hypotheses. Their theorems concern moments and limiting distributions. The criterion here locates every zero at each finite size. [Liu–Yin, arXiv:2503.02990v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02990v2)

These results are reported with their mathematical scope, without a claim of absolute priority.
